# PDFuse: Stop Uploading Sensitive PDFs to Random Websites

PDFuse is a Chrome extension that merges, splits and edits PDFs, turns images into PDFs and exports PDF pages as images, inside your browser. It is for anyone who handles sensitive documents and does not want to upload them: your files are never uploaded, stored on a server, or sent to anyone.

PDFuse splits, merges, and manipulates PDFs directly in Chrome—your documents never leave your browser. Fast, private, secure.

No signup. Setup in under 2 minutes.
